Illumineer's Trove

Illumineer's Trove Lore Counters

Format: All official Ravensburger lore counters are cardboard spin-dial wheels — two concentric discs with a rotating outer ring displaying numbers 0–20. Sets 1 and 2 Trove boxes did not include a lore counter; all sets from Set 3 onward include one.

League & OP Prize

League & Organized Play Prize Counters

Sets 1–6: Counters were awarded to the top-6 point earners at the end of each 12-week League season (one counter per kit, 6 per season).
Sets 7+: The point-based League was replaced by weekly events. Counters became new-player welcome gifts given to anyone playing their first Disney Lorcana store event.
