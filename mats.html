Convention Prize Mats

Awarded to top finishers at Ravensburger-run convention tournaments (UK Games Expo, Gen Con, etc.). One new mat released per year. Not awarded at Lorcana Challenge events.

🎲
Convention Prize
2023
2023 Convention Playmat

Six characters — one per ink: Moana (Amber), Mickey Mouse (Amethyst), Cruella De Vil (Emerald), Aladdin (Ruby), Maleficent (Sapphire), Simba (Steel). Top 8 finishers at the Gen Con 2023 Finale. Some copies hand-signed by the Lorcana design team. First-ever Lorcana convention mat.

Gen Con 2023 Aug 2023
🎭
Convention Prize

2024 Convention Playmat

Six characters from Sets 2–3: Chernabog, HeiHei, Belle – Hidden Archer, Jim Hawkins, Alice – Growing Girl, Kida – Royal Warrior. Awarded at UK Games Expo (May 2024), Scotland Card Show (Jun 2024), and Gen Con 2024 (Aug 2024).

Multi-Event 2024 May–Aug 2024

Lorcana Challenge Circuit Mats

The Challenge is Lorcana's main competitive circuit. Mats are earned via Prize Wall tickets or awarded to top cut finishers. Season 1: May 2024 – Jun 2025. Season 2: late 2025 onward.

👠

Prize Wall
Cinderella – Stouthearted ✦ Foil

Challenge-exclusive alt art, foil finish. The most coveted Season 1 Prize Wall item. Cost approx. 90–100 Prize Wall tickets. Available at Atlanta, Chicago, and subsequent Challenge events.
